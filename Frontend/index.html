OUR PURPOSE

About SMRITI

When Memory Fades, Care Remains.

SMRITI is a healthcare assistance concept designed to support caregivers and individuals experiencing memory difficulties. By bringing medication tracking, visitor awareness, and safety monitoring together, SMRITI aims to make everyday care more organized and reassuring.

Our goal is not to replace human care, but to explore how technology can help caregivers stay informed, manage daily routines, and respond to potential safety concerns.

Our belief: Technology should support care, preserve dignity, and help people feel safer.

UNDERSTANDING MEMORY

Understanding Alzheimer's Disease

Alzheimer's disease is a progressive brain disorder that affects memory, thinking, and everyday activities. Its effects change over time and vary from person to person.

Hippocampus

Plays an important role in forming new memories.

Entorhinal Cortex

🧩

How does Alzheimer's affect the brain?

Alzheimer's commonly affects memory-related structures, including the hippocampus and entorhinal cortex, early in the disease. As it progresses, other brain regions can also become affected.

It is not limited to one isolated point in the brain.

💚

Why does this matter to SMRITI?

Memory difficulties can make daily routines more challenging. Remembering medication schedules, maintaining familiar routines, and communicating changing needs may require additional support.

SMRITI explores how digital tools could help caregivers organize daily care and review potential safety concerns.

HONESTY BY DESIGN

Feature Transparency

SMRITI is a developing prototype. We clearly distinguish working functionality from demonstrations and planned features.

Implemented

Medication Management

  • Medicine scheduling
  • Multiple daily dose times
  • Dose-status recording
  • Browser-based data persistence

Medication data is stored in the browser. Dose status is recorded by the user or caregiver; it does not confirm that medicine was swallowed.

Demonstration

Visitor & Safety Awareness

  • Sample visitor history
  • Recognition settings interface
  • Simulated safety alerts
  • Demonstration alert management

Visitor records are sample data. Safety alerts are simulated. Live facial recognition and real-world event detection are not connected.

Planned

Connected Safety Features

  • Live facial recognition
  • GPS and geofencing
  • Remote caregiver notifications
  • Emergency contact workflows

These features require additional development, appropriate device permissions, connected services, and privacy safeguards.

🛡️

A note on care and safety

SMRITI is a prototype designed to explore caregiver support. It is not a medical diagnostic tool and does not replace professional healthcare or appropriate human supervision. Safety events should always be verified by a caregiver.
